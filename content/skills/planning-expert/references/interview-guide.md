# Pre-Coding Interview Guide

## Phase 1: Understand Need
1. What problem are we solving? (in user's words)
2. Who benefits? (role/persona)
3. What does success look like? (measurable)

## Phase 2: Scope Boundaries
1. What is explicitly IN scope?
2. What is explicitly OUT of scope?
3. What are the constraints? (time, budget, tech)

## Phase 3: Technical Deep-Dive
1. What existing systems must this integrate with?
2. Are there data or schema changes needed?
3. What are the security/compliance requirements?
4. What are the performance targets? (p95 < Xms, N TPS)
5. Any regulatory concerns? (data residency, accessibility compliance, export controls)

## Red Flags
- "We'll figure it out later" -> undefined requirements
- "Make it fast" -> unquantified (needs numbers)
- "While we're at it, also add..." -> scope creep
- "Just copy what X does" -> unvalidated assumptions
